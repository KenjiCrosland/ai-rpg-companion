Senses darkvision 120 ft.; Passive Perception 19
Languages understands Common and Sylvan but doesn't speak
CR 16 (XP 15,000; PB +5)
Traits
Legendary Resistance (3/Day). If the mother fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.
Gentle Grasp. The mother can climb difficult surfaces, including along ceilings, without needing to make an ability check.
Web Sense. While in contact with a web, the mother knows the exact location of any other creature in contact with that web.
Absorbent Body. When the mother takes damage from a melee attack, it reduces that damage by 11 (2d10).
Actions
Multiattack. The mother makes three attacks: two Pseudopod attacks and one Web Lash attack.
Pseudopod. Melee Attack Roll: +12, reach 5 ft. 29 (5d8 + 7) Bludgeoning damage.
Web Lash. Melee Attack Roll: +12, reach 20 ft. 29 (5d8 + 7) Bludgeoning damage. If the target is a Large or smaller creature, it has the Restrained condition until the start of the mother's next turn.
Web Blast (Recharge 5–6). Dexterity Saving Throw: DC 20, each creature in a 30-foot Cone. Failure: 44 (8d10) Bludgeoning damage, and the target has the Restrained condition until the start of the mother's next turn. Success: Half damage only.
Sorrowful Bark. The mother weaves a protective web around itself or one ally it can see within 30 feet. Until the start of the mother's next turn, the target gains a +3 bonus to AC.
Reactions
Tender Ward. Trigger: A creature the mother can see within 30 feet hits an ally of the mother with an attack roll. Response: The mother interposes itself, reducing the damage dealt to that ally by 22 (5d8).
Legendary Actions
Legendary Action Uses: 3. Immediately after another creature's turn, it can expend a use to take one of the following actions. It regains all expended uses at the start of each of its turns.
Pseudopod. The mother makes one Pseudopod attack.
Shift Web. The mother moves up to its speed without provoking Opportunity Attacks, and it can move through spaces occupied by Restrained creatures.
