Traits
Slurp. When in dim light or darkness, the sussk can take the Hide action as a Bonus Action. While motionless in darkness, it is Invisible.
Seeping Venom. When the sussk hits a creature that is surprised or hasn't taken a turn in combat with its Venom Strike, the target takes an extra 44 (8d10) Poison damage and has the Poisoned condition until the end of the sussk's next turn.
Abyssal Pressure. The sussk has Advantage on saving throws against spells and magical effects originating from the Material Plane.
Actions
Multiattack. The sussk makes two attacks: one with its Bite and one with its Coil.
Bite. Melee Attack Roll: +11, reach 5 ft. 21 (4d6 + 7) Piercing damage plus 4 (1d8) Fire damage.
Coil. Melee Attack Roll: +11, reach 10 ft. 21 (4d6 + 7) Bludgeoning damage. If the target is Medium or smaller, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 17) and the Restrained condition until the grapple ends.
Venom Strike. Ranged Attack Roll: +11, range 60 ft. 7 (1d6 + 4) Poison damage. Constitution Saving Throw: DC 17, one creature within 30 feet of the target. Failure: 22 (5d8) Poison damage. Success: Half damage.
Bonus Actions
Shadow Slip. The sussk teleports up to 30 feet to an unoccupied space it can see that is in dim light or darkness.
