Crystal Shards. Ranged Attack Roll: +10, range 60 ft. 46 (9d8 + 6) Piercing damage.
Shatter Cone. Dexterity Saving Throw: DC 17, each creature in a 30-foot Cone. Failure: 45 (13d6) Slashing damage. Success: Half damage.
Bonus Actions
Phase Leap. The slammer moves up to its speed. This movement does not provoke Opportunity Attacks.
