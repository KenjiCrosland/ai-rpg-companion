Traits
Midnight Hag. The spore scuttler has advantage on Intelligence saving throws.
Dusk Hound. The spore scuttler has advantage on attack rolls against a creature if at least one of the scuttler's allies is within 5 feet of that creature and the ally isn't Incapacitated.
Actions
Multiattack. The spore scuttler makes two attacks: one with its Leg Swipe and one with its Mind Nip.
Leg Swipe. Melee Attack Roll: +9, reach 5 ft. 29 (7d6 + 5) Slashing damage. Strength Saving Throw: DC 16, the target. Failure: The target is pushed up to 10 feet horizontally away from the scuttler.
Mind Nip. Ranged Attack Roll: +9, range 60 ft. 29 (7d6 + 5) Psychic damage. Intelligence Saving Throw: DC 16, the target. Failure: Until the start of the scuttler's next turn, the target's speed is halved and it can't take reactions.
Spore Burst (Recharge 5-6). Constitution Saving Throw: DC 16, each creature in a 20-foot-radius Sphere centered on a point the scuttler can see within 60 feet. Failure: 31 (9d6) Poison damage. Success: Half damage. The area is lightly obscured for 1 minute.
Bonus Actions
Skittering Retreat. The spore scuttler takes the Disengage action.
