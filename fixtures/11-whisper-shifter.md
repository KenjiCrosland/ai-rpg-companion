Languages understands Primordial but does not speak
CR 23 (XP 50,000; PB +7)
Traits
Unseen Lurker. The whisper shifter is Invisible while it remains motionless and has not taken damage since the end of its last turn.
Sinkhole Snap. Dexterity Saving Throw: DC 23, each creature within 10 feet of its arrival point. Failure: 28 (8d6) Bludgeoning damage and the target has the Prone condition. Success: Half damage.
Legendary Resistance (3/Day). If the whisper shifter fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.
Actions
Multiattack. The whisper shifter makes three Dust-Caked Claw attacks.
Dust-Caked Claw. Melee Attack Roll: +17, reach 5 ft. 37 (6d8 + 10) Slashing damage. If the target is a Medium or smaller creature, the whisper shifter moves it up to 10 feet horizontally, and the target has the Prone condition.
Grit Blast. Ranged Attack Roll: +17, range 60 ft. 28 (4d8 + 10) Bludgeoning damage, plus 7 (2d6) Thunder damage.
Buried Grasp. Strength Saving Throw: DC 23, one creature on the ground within 30 feet. Failure: 55 (10d10) Bludgeoning damage, and the target has the Restrained condition. The whisper shifter may maintain the Restrained condition on subsequent turns by using a bonus action; otherwise the condition ends at the end of the whisper shifter's next turn. Success: Half damage.
Nova: Displacement Detonation. The whisper shifter becomes visible as it unleashes a concussive blast. Constitution Saving Throw: DC 23, each creature within 20 feet. Failure: 72 (16d8) Thunder damage, and the target is pushed 20 feet away from the whisper shifter and has the Prone condition. Success: Half damage and the target is not pushed or knocked Prone. After using this action, the whisper shifter is no longer Invisible (regardless of any trait). This action can be used only once per day.
Legendary Actions
Legendary Action Uses: 3. Immediately after another creature's turn, it can expend a use to take one of the following actions. It regains all expended uses at the start of each of its turns.
Claw Lunge. The whisper shifter moves up to its Fly speed without provoking Opportunity Attacks, then makes one Dust-Caked Claw attack.
Buried Grasp. The whisper shifter uses Buried Grasp.
