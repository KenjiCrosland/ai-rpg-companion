CR 14 (XP 11,500; PB +5)
Traits
Pale Shardweaver. The shardweaver's jagged crystal form reflects light and magic. It has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects, and it takes no damage from difficult terrain caused by nonmagical earth or stone.
Legendary Resistance (3/Day). If the shardweaver fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.
Shardheart Aura. At the start of the shardweaver's turn, each creature within 10 feet of it takes 5 (1d10) Piercing damage. A creature that hits the shardweaver with a melee attack takes 5 (1d10) Piercing damage.
Actions
Multiattack. The shardweaver makes three Crystal Slam attacks.
Crystal Slam. Melee Attack Roll: +11, reach 5 ft. 20 (4d6 + 6) Piercing damage.
Crystal Spire Burst. Dexterity Saving Throw: DC 19, each creature in a 30-foot Cone. Failure: 22 (5d8) Piercing damage, and the target has the Restrained condition until the end of the shardweaver's next turn. Success: Half damage only.
Wall of Shards (Recharge 5-6). The shardweaver raises a wall of jagged crystal in a line 60 feet long, 10 feet high, and 5 feet thick within 60 feet of itself. The wall lasts for 1 minute or until the shardweaver uses this action again. The wall provides half cover, and a creature that moves through the wall's space takes 11 (2d10) Piercing damage for every 5 feet moved. The wall has AC 17, 50 Hit Points, and immunity to Poison and Psychic damage.
Shatter Ground (1/Day). The shardweaver slams the ground, turning the area within a 40-foot-radius Sphere centered on itself into difficult terrain of crystal shards. A creature that enters that area for the first time on a turn or starts its turn there takes 11 (2d10) Piercing damage. The shardweaver is unaffected. The effect lasts for 1 minute.
Legendary Actions
Legendary Action Uses: 3. Immediately after another creature's turn, it can expend a use to take one of the following actions. It regains all expended uses at the start of each of its turns.
Slam. The shardweaver makes one Crystal Slam attack.
Crystal Growth. The shardweaver causes a burst of crystals in a 10-foot-radius sphere centered on a point it can see within 60 feet. Each creature in that area must succeed on a DC 19 Dexterity saving throw or take 7 (2d6) Piercing damage and have its speed reduced by 10 feet until the end of its next turn.
