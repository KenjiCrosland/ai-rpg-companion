Traits
Swarm. The swarm can occupy another creature's space and vice versa, and the swarm can move through any opening large enough for a Tiny creature. The swarm can't regain Hit Points or gain Temporary Hit Points.
Legendary Resistance (3/Day). If the Skittering Hive fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.
Amorphous. The Skittering Hive can move through a space as narrow as 1 inch wide without squeezing.
Telepathic Hive. The Skittering Hive can communicate telepathically with any creature within 120 feet that can understand a language. It can also sense the emotions and surface thoughts of any creature within 30 feet.
Actions
Multiattack. The Skittering Hive makes three Swarm of Psychic Bites attacks.
Swarm of Psychic Bites. Melee Attack Roll: +15, reach 5 ft. 56 (12d8 + 2) Psychic damage, or half as much damage if the swarm has half of its Hit Points or fewer. Wisdom Saving Throw: DC 23, one creature. Failure: The target has the Stunned condition until the end of its next turn.
Psychic Pulse. Intelligence Saving Throw: DC 23, each creature in a 20-foot-radius sphere centered on a point within 120 feet. Failure: 84 (13d12) Psychic damage. Success: Half damage.
Coiling Grasp. Strength Saving Throw: DC 23, one creature within 30 feet. Failure: The target has the Restrained condition. At the start of each of the target's turns, it takes 22 (4d10) Psychic damage. Success: None.
Legendary Actions
Legendary Action Uses: 3. Immediately after another creature's turn, it can expend a use to take one of the following actions. It regains all expended uses at the start of each of its turns.
Psychic Bite. The Skittering Hive makes one Swarm of Psychic Bites attack.
Psychic Pulse. The Skittering Hive uses Psychic Pulse.
Coiling Grasp. The Skittering Hive uses Coiling Grasp.
