Shusgh
Large aberration, neutral evil
Armor Class20
Hit Points: 252 (24d10 + 120)
Speed: 20 ft., burrow 20 ft.
STR	DEX	CON	INT	WIS	CHA
28 (+9)	10 (+0)	20 (+5)	7 (-2)	16 (+3)	6 (-2)
Skills: Perception +9, Stealth +6
Saving Throws: CON +11, WIS +9
Damage Resistances: bludgeoning, piercing
Damage Immunities: acid
Condition Immunities: poisoned
Senses: blindsight 60 ft., tremorsense 120 ft.; passive Perception 19
Languages: understands Deep Speech but cannot speak
CR:19 (22,000 XP)
Proficiency Bonus:+6
Phasing Passage. The shusgh can move through solid stone, earth, and flesh as if they were difficult terrain. If it ends its turn inside a solid object, it is shunted to the nearest unoccupied space and takes 10 (3d6) force damage.
Ethereal Glow. The shusgh sheds dim light in a 10-foot radius. Attacks made against it while it is in dim light or darkness have advantage.
Crude Intelligence. The shusgh has advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on scent or vibration, and it cannot be surprised while it is aware of any creature within its tremorsense range.
Legendary Resistance (3/Day). If the shusgh fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.
Actions
Multiattack: The shusgh makes three attacks: one with its paralyzing sting and two with its phased tendril.
Paralyzing Sting: Melee Weapon Attack: +15 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 23 (4d6 + 9) piercing damage, and the target must succeed on a DC 21 Constitution saving throw or be paralyzed for 1 minute. The target can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on a success.
Phased Tendril: Melee Weapon Attack: +15 to hit, reach 15 ft., one target. Hit: 18 (2d8 + 9) force damage.
Vulg'oth Gush (Recharge 5-6): The shusgh releases a burst of psychic energy from its body. Each creature within 20 feet of it must make a DC 21 Wisdom saving throw, taking 36 (8d8) psychic damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one. A creature that fails the saving throw is also stunned until the end of its next turn.
Burrowing Ambush: The shusgh burrows up to its burrow speed without provoking opportunity attacks. If it emerges within 5 feet of a creature, it can make one Paralyzing Sting attack with advantage. This attack, if it hits, deals an extra 49 (14d6) piercing damage.
