Traits
Amorphous. The jellypack can move through a space as narrow as 1 inch without expending extra movement to do so.
Scuttling Detach. The jellypack can take the Disengage action as a Bonus Action.
Actions
Multiattack. The jellypack makes two Pseudopod attacks.
Pseudopod. Melee Attack Roll: +7, reach 5 ft. 17 (3d8 + 4) Acid damage.
Dissolve Armor. Dexterity Saving Throw: DC 14, one creature within 30 feet. Failure: 18 (4d8) Acid damage, and the target's Armor Class is reduced by 1 until the end of its next turn. Success: Half damage only.
Engulf (Recharge 5-6). Strength Saving Throw: DC 14, one Medium or smaller creature within 5 feet. Failure: The target is pulled into the jellypack's space, has the Grappled condition (escape DC 14), and takes 9 (2d8) Acid damage at the start of each of the jellypack's turns. The jellypack can't move while grappling, and it can have only one creature Grappled in this way at a time.
