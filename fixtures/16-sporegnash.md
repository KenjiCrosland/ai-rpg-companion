Traits
Legendary Resistance (3/Day). If the sporegnash fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.
Spore Drift. Constitution Saving Throw: DC 24, each creature. Failure: The creature has the Poisoned condition until the end of its next turn and cannot take Reactions while Poisoned in this way.
Unearthly Cunning. The sporegnash has Advantage on Intelligence saving throws and ability checks.
Actions
Multiattack. The sporegnash makes three Frost-Claw attacks and can use Spore Burst or Vexing Phantasm in place of one attack.
Frost-Claw. Melee Attack Roll: +15, reach 5 ft. 39 (7d8 + 8) Piercing damage plus 4 (1d8) Cold damage.
Spore Burst. Constitution Saving Throw: DC 24, each creature in a 20-foot-radius sphere centered on a point within 60 feet. Failure: 59 (17d6) Poison damage and the target has the Stunned condition until the end of its next turn. Success: Half damage only.
Vexing Phantasm. Wisdom Saving Throw: DC 24, one creature within 60 feet. Failure: The target perceives illusory duplicates that harry its attacks. Until the start of the sporegnash's next turn, attack rolls against the target have Advantage, and the target's attack rolls have Disadvantage. Success: The effect ends at the end of the current turn.
Frost Spore Shard. Ranged Attack Roll: +15, range 60 ft. 39 (7d8 + 8) Cold damage. If the target is a creature, its Speed is reduced by 20 feet until the start of the sporegnash's next turn.
Legendary Actions
Legendary Action Uses: 3. Immediately after another creature's turn, it can expend a use to take one of the following actions. It regains all expended uses at the start of each of its turns.
Claw. The sporegnash makes one Frost-Claw attack.
Vexing Phantasm. The sporegnash uses Vexing Phantasm.
Spore Drift. The sporegnash moves up to its Speed without provoking Opportunity Attacks. Until the end of its turn, its Spore Drift trait extends to a 10-foot Emanation centered on itself.
