Traits
False Appearance. While the silent shard remains motionless, it is indistinguishable from a natural crystal formation.
Legendary Resistance (3/Day). If the silent shard fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.
Magic Resistance. The silent shard has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.
Actions
Multiattack. The silent shard makes three attacks: two Shard Projection attacks and one Crystal Claw attack. It can replace one Shard Projection attack with a use of Shard Storm.
Crystal Claw. Melee Attack Roll: +18, reach 5 ft. 95 (19d8 + 10) Slashing damage.
Shard Projection. Ranged Attack Roll: +18, range 60 ft. 95 (19d8 + 10) Slashing damage.
Shard Storm. Dexterity Saving Throw: DC 25, each creature in a 20-foot-radius sphere centered on a point within 120 feet. Failure: 143 (22d12) Slashing damage. Success: Half damage.
Bonus Actions
Hush Horror. Dexterity Saving Throw: DC 25, each creature within 5 feet of the space it left. Failure: 22 (4d10) Slashing damage.
Legendary Actions
Legendary Action Uses: 3. Immediately after another creature's turn, it can expend a use to take one of the following actions. It regains all expended uses at the start of each of its turns.
Shard Volley. The silent shard makes one Shard Projection attack.
Lull Shudder. The silent shard teleports up to 30 feet to an unoccupied space it can see.
Shatter. The silent shard makes one Crystal Claw attack. On a hit, Strength Saving Throw: DC 25, one creature. Failure: The target has the Prone condition.
