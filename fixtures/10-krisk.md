Traits
Legendary Resistance (3/Day). If the krisk fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.
Amorphous Shards. The krisk can move through spaces as narrow as 1 inch without squeezing. A creature that starts its turn in the krisk's space takes 13 (2d12) Slashing damage.
Flyby. The krisk doesn't provoke Opportunity Attacks when it flies out of an enemy's reach.
Snap Surge. The krisk has Advantage on an attack roll against a creature if at least one of the krisk's allies is within 5 feet of the creature and the ally doesn't have the Incapacitated condition.
Actions
Multiattack. The krisk makes three Shard Strike attacks.
Shard Strike. Melee Attack Roll: +19, reach 5 ft. 100 (20d8 + 10) Slashing damage.
Shatter Armor (Recharge 5-6). Strength Saving Throw: DC 26, each creature in a 30-foot Cone. Failure: 149 (23d12) Slashing damage and the target has its Armor Class reduced by 3 for 1 minute (or until a creature uses an action to remove the shattered fragments). Success: Half damage only.
Shred Shard. Ranged Attack Roll: +19, range 60 ft. 100 (20d8 + 10) Slashing damage.
Slip Spiral. The krisk flies up to half its Speed without provoking Opportunity Attacks, then makes one Shard Strike attack.
Legendary Actions
Legendary Action Uses: 3. Immediately after another creature's turn, it can expend a use to take one of the following actions. It regains all expended uses at the start of each of its turns.
Flit. The krisk flies up to its Speed without provoking Opportunity Attacks.
Shatter Armor. The krisk uses Shatter Armor, if available.
Stinging Shard. The krisk makes one Shred Shard attack.
