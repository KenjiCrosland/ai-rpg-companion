Traits
Legendary Resistance (3/Day). If the Lasher fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.
Elusive. The Lasher can take the Disengage action as a bonus action.
Sand Swim. The Lasher can burrow through sand and loose earth without disturbing the surface.
Telepathy. The Lasher can communicate telepathically with any creature within 120 feet that has a language.
Actions
Multiattack. The Lasher makes three attacks using Spectral Lash or Sand Bolt, in any combination.
Spectral Lash. Melee Attack Roll: +12, reach 5 ft. 31 (7d6 + 7) Psychic damage.
Sand Bolt. Ranged Attack Roll: +12, range 60 ft. 31 (7d6 + 7) Force damage.
Sand Blast (Recharge 5–6). Constitution Saving Throw: DC 19, each creature in a 20-foot-radius sphere centered on a point within 60 feet. Failure: 49 (14d6) Slashing damage. Success: Half damage.
Mind Trick. Wisdom Saving Throw: DC 19, one creature within 60 feet. Failure: The target has the Charmed condition for 1 minute. While Charmed, the target follows the Lasher’s simple verbal commands and cannot take reactions. The target repeats the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on a success.
Legendary Actions
Legendary Action Uses: 3. Immediately after another creature's turn, it can expend a use to take one of the following actions. It regains all expended uses at the start of each of its turns.
Lash. The Lasher makes one Spectral Lash attack.
Glide. The Lasher moves up to its speed without provoking Opportunity Attacks.
Confound. Wisdom Saving Throw: DC 19, one creature within 60 feet. Failure: The target has the Stunned condition until the end of its next turn. Success: The target has the Frightened condition until the end of its next turn.
