## Owner's generalization — the body/controlled-medium dichotomy as a CLASSIFICATION field

Owner, 2026-11-10, while item B was being built:

> "grapple/restrain mechanisms don't always need to be in body. The creature can create or
> manipulate something they control which has the same effect"
> "in fact that dichotomy might be useful in classification"

> "there's a roper, and then there's some sort of 'mudbender' that manipulates surrounding mud to do
> the same thing" · "maybe 3 shapes?" · "And of course there is just grippy teeth" ·
> "so potentially 4 shapes"

**FOUR SHAPES OF A HOLD.** Each implies a different ROW, which is why this is better than a yes/no —
the shape decides reach, area and whether a containment clause exists at all:

| shape | what holds | the row it wants | print (measured 2026-11-10) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **grip** | jaws, claws, a grab at touch | Grappled rider on a melee attack, reach 5–10 | **48 rows** — Ankheg, Bugbear Grab, Barbed Devil |
| **reach** | a body part that extends | a grapple attack at 15–60 ft., then a Reel | **4 rows** — Roper (60 ft.), Kraken (30), Aboleth (15), Tarrasque (15) |
| **contain** | the target goes INSIDE | the engulf engine: blinded, restrained, Total Cover, a tick, an exit | **11 rows** — Swallow ×6, Engulf ×2, Smother, Whelm, and the Giant Frog at Medium |
| **medium** | a mass it creates or commands | a zone/area restrain whose fiction is the medium, no anatomy | **0 rows — print has none** |

**The medium shape has no print instance.** The Water Elemental whelms because it IS water; the
Shambling Mound engulfs because it IS the vegetation. A "mudbender" that commands mud it is not made
of is an INVENTION — permitted by the owner's own ruling ("print is a resource, not a veto: invent one
that fits if none exists"), but it has no calibration peer, so its numbers must be borrowed from the
nearest analogue (the contain rows for a swallow-like hold, the area rows for a zone).

**What item B did instead, and why it is only half the answer.** Item B asks the body-verdict call a
single bespoke yes/no for the engulf engine (`ENGULF_BODY_REQUIREMENT`, `templates.mjs`). That works,
costs no extra round trip, and is measured — but the question is asked once, for one mechanism, and
thrown away. Every other hold mechanism re-derives the same fact or ignores it: the route call's
`holds` read, the grapple templates, `brute-grappler` donor selection, and the restrain riders.

**The proposal.** Ask it ONCE on the classification/resolve call as a field — e.g.
`hold_shape: grip | reach | contain | medium | none` — and let every consumer read it:
- the engulf engine ships only on `own_body` or `controlled_medium` (replacing item B's bespoke verdict);
- a `controlled_medium` answer licenses a restrain/engulf row whose fiction is the medium, not anatomy,
  which is the case item B's first wording wrongly refused (a mage's grasping vines; print's own
  Shambling Mound engulfs as a Plant);
- `neither` means a hold is a plain grapple and the containing half never appears.

**Evidence to gather before building it.** Item B's verdict answer is now traced on every sheet that
carries an engulf engine (`engulf engine withheld:` / `body verdicts:`). Run a forty, then count how
often the verdict's yes/no would have differed from a three-way field — specifically how many sheets
are `controlled_medium` creatures that the yes/no had to approve for the wrong reason. If that number
is small, the bespoke verdict is enough and this stays unbuilt; if GMs routinely type
command-the-medium concepts, the field earns its place.

**This is a prompt change to the classification call: ask the owner before writing it.**
