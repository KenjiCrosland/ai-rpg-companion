# Vocabulary authority redesign — accumulated observations

**Trigger: the facet/house authority split (audit finding #2), or any
texture-bank authoring pass.**

- One authority per field: houses own MATERIAL words (substances,
  restraints, terrain, material name-words); facets own BEHAVIORAL words
  (deliveries, appendages, manner adjectives) and damage affinity. 16 of 25
  houses currently collide with facet vet_tokens (patched by making house
  tokens always admissible, 2026-08-11 — the underlying dual authority
  remains).
- House NOUN banks skew OBJECT (iron: Anvil, Rivet, Bellows, Engine;
  light: Censer...). Fed to the flavor call as "name words," they produce
  object-headed CREATURE names — "Gilded Censer" for a porcelain eel
  (live, 2026-08-14). DeepSeek already clusters toward animated objects;
  the banks amplify it. Mark nouns agent vs object, and surface the
  object-head rule (currently naming-pass-only) wherever creature names
  are minted.
- Fey + light-house vocabulary (gilded/haloed/censer) reads as
  celestial-construct register. Consider register notes per type on the
  house rows.
