# The owner's GM read of save10-2026-10-27 — verdict, what landed, what is open

**2 ship / 6 fix-lite / 2 fix.** The full read is in the session transcript; this
file carries the verdict, the two fixes that landed, and the open items in the
owner's own "Done when" terms. Read it before touching signature PLACEMENT or
row NAMING.

## Landed (committed with this plan)

**1. A payoff never imposes the condition it requires — the clause is STRIPPED.**
`condition-payoff` asks its target to already HAVE a condition and then imposes a
minor one, and nothing stopped both being the same:

> Killing Cold Wake. **Strength** Saving Throw: DC 19, one creature within 60
> feet that has the Prone condition. Failure: 84 (13d12) Cold damage, **and the
> target has the Prone condition.**

The second clause can never do anything, and A2 then read that dead
`imposes: Prone` and paired it with print's Strength on a row whose point is 84
Cold damage. Now:

> Dexterity Saving Throw: DC 18, one creature within 60 feet that has the Prone
> condition. Failure: 84 (13d12) Cold damage. Success: Half damage.

Swapping in a DIFFERENT condition was the first cut and was worse — it put a
Poisoned rider on a cold row. A payoff's reward is its damage; the rider is
optional, so where it would be vestigial the clause leaves.

**2. No Strength save on a damage-dominant row.** Strength was in the acceptable
set until a 67-damage poison fly-by (Plague Dive) and the 84-damage cold payoff
both shipped on it: the Prone rider named the stat and the damage was ignored.
print's one Strength save on an area-damage-plus-Prone row is the Centaur
Trooper's Trampling Charge, which MOVES its target — already the `movement` arm,
which returns Strength first. So a row that shoves or hauls keeps Strength; a row
whose point is damage does not.

Replay: `__tests__/payoff-vestigial-condition.spec.js` (7 cases, both real rows).
Suite 4,011, build clean.

**3. The name leak is now countable.** A trace fires when a row ships with its
template's own default name because the call named nothing the validator would
take. Measured over the save10 runs of 2026-10-26/27: **16 of 111 templated rows
(14%)**. Not patched — see below.

## Open, in the owner's terms

### "No template name reaches a sheet unreskinned" — NAMING
The 16 split two ways. Some defaults are print's own and read fine on the right
body (`Vile Appearance` on a dread sovereign, `Aberrant Ground` on an ooze). The
rest read as catalog furniture on the wrong creature: **Answering Sting** on a
spearman with no sting, **Tolling Drain** on a bell ghost (neither a toll nor a
drain), **Venomed Edge** as a bludgeoning buff on a timber golem, **Crownbolt** a
melee row named like a bolt, **Soul Rupture** for what the GM called a peal.

The cure is the fit call naming the row — it is asked for `name` and the
validator takes 2-3 words, Title Case, no digits, not the creature's own name.
Deriving a name in the engine would be the engine inventing fiction, so this is
a PROMPT question: find why 14% come back unusable (absent? rejected by the
3-word cap? refused for containing the creature's name?) by logging the raw
answer beside the rejection, then fix the ask. Do NOT add a name generator.

Also shipped: **"Shoulder-Seige Golem"** — a misspelling in a creature's NAME,
from the naming call. And **"hurls a hurled object"** in a Cinder Rounds row.
Both are call output; the second looks like a slot filled with a phrase that
already contained the verb.

### "Template placement" — the biggest open item
Three asks that should have been keys and were not:
- "whose hammer finds the terrified" → a payoff on Frightened. The clause call
  read it `[asked: attack]`. **The GM typed the loop and the sheet does not have
  it** — the single worst miss in the read.
- "plague-bearing archon" → contagion. Landed unasked on a wolf and a skeleton in
  earlier runs; missed here.
- "shoulders through walls" → siege. No Siege Monster, no wall trait, for the
  third variant of that concept.

And the inverse: payoff-on-condition landed unasked 4x across two sweeps;
`assassinate` landed on a leviathan and an archon, neither of which ambushes.
This is the resolve/clause call's mechanic vocabulary, not the fit menu — the fit
menu now orders correctly on stamps. Next step is to read what `ASKED_MECHANICS`
offers against these three phrasings before changing anything.

### "Rolling wall is a Line"
A "rolling wall of thunder" shipped as a 15-foot Cone. Print's wall is a Line.
The area shape comes from the call's `area_shape` ∈ the template's `targets`;
check whether Line was offered for that template and the call chose Cone, or
whether Line was never on the menu.
