# Step 7 brief — named parts are engine rows; the template path composes; authoring is the last resort — 2026-10-11

Supersedes the first draft of this date (threshold rule). Owner's question that reshaped it: "why do we need authoring if someone names claw or greatsword — that feels like it can be part of the existing template engine?" It can. What authoring decided for Meshrin's four parts was the name, the damage type, the mode and a rider; the engine already owns the first three and a small table owns the fourth. The freer call was also the source of the check-in's text defects (a stare priced as a melee attack, "a 15 Charisma saving throw", a flavor sentence as a trait) — hand-written template rows never produce those.

## The finding this brief answers

Step 5 made the authoring call the composition for any sheet with one mechanical clause and silenced the trait seat and the signature path on such a sheet. The twelve-block check-in (`CHECKIN-template-path-2026-10-11.md`) shows the cost: the one-liners lost Sure Footing, Aura of Authority, the drain, the exploiter and the rally; verdicts went worse on four of six and better on none; with the authoring call refused, the same prompts reproduced the October 4 shapes and read better on four of five. Authoring's one real win — Meshrin's greatsword, tail, claws and bite as rows with a Multiattack that names them — is deterministic work.

## Design

Three moves, in this order of precedence on a sheet:

### 1. Named parts are engine rows (no call)

After the rename pass, every clause in the record that names a body part or weapon (the `PART` list in `groundedClauses`: sword, greatsword, axe, spear, lance, bow, crossbow, dagger, staff, mace, hammer, club, whip, net, tail, claws, teeth, fangs, jaws, horns, hooves, wings, tentacles, talons, stinger, beak, lantern, shield…) becomes an attack row:

- **name**: the user's phrase, title-cased and trimmed of its verb ("has razor sharp claws" → Razor Claws; "large teeth for a vicious bite" → Vicious Bite; "wields a greatsword" → Greatsword). A phrase with two parts ("claws and a vicious bite") is two rows.
- **numbers**: a clone of the chassis's priced attack slot (the first part takes the slot itself, as the authoring mapping did; the rest clone it). The attack cap stays (dragon 5, else `max(3, slots + 2)`); past the cap, parts fold into the last row's flavor. The final price pass re-solves the round as today.
- **type**: `TYPE_BY_NOUN` (greatsword Slashing, tail Bludgeoning, bite Piercing, lantern Fire…); a type word in the same phrase outranks the noun ("a tail that burns" → Fire).
- **mode**: bow, crossbow, sling, javelin, dart, net, lantern (thrown) → ranged, 30/120 or the weapon's print range; everything else melee at the body's reach.
- **rider**: a small noun table only — tail, hooves, slam → Prone; jaws, tentacles, maw → Grappled with the escape DC in print's form; stinger → Poisoned; net → Restrained (escape DC). No other riders from this pass. A condition word in the phrase ("a tail that stuns") is honoured from the closed list.
- **Multiattack**: rewritten to name every part ("makes two attacks, using Greatsword, Tail Swipe, Razor Claws or Vicious Bite in any combination"; one part → "makes N X attacks"). The 2014 renderer is `multiattack2014` (defect 1 below fixed).
- a part row carries `clause` (the user's phrase) and `part: true`; the identical-row dedupe exempts part rows.

Trace: `named parts: Greatsword (slot), Tail Swipe (clone, Prone), Razor Claws (clone), Vicious Bite (clone) — the user's words, the engine's numbers`.

### 2. The template path composes everything else, untouched

The trait seat, the idea and fit calls for the free slot, the library fallback, slot yield, the value check — all run as they did at the release, on every sheet. `authoredSheet` and its skips are deleted. Part rows take attack slots and nothing else; the signature slot stays open for the concept's verb as before, so "is trained in fighting styles" goes to the idea and fit calls and lands where the step-5 live Meshrin landed it (the condition payoff on the tail's Prone). A clause a part row already claims (its words in the row's name) is not offered to the idea call as a second element.

### 3. Authoring is the repair call, last, and strict

One call, after the template path and the final derivations, only for a mechanical clause that no part row, template row or seed claimed (the step-6 "unclaimed" test, by row attribution and name, never chassis prose). Same forms; the sheet as context; new rows only. With the strictness below (defects 5–7) and never on a print body. Most sheets never reach it; Meshrin-shaped prompts reach it only if the template path left a clause on the floor. The pre-rename authoring block, `buildAuthorPrompt`, `landAuthored`'s slot mapping and the same-form yield code go; `buildRepairPrompt`, `parseAuthorResponse` and the landing of new rows stay.

Cost: two calls fewer on every one-liner; one fewer on Meshrin-shaped prompts (the idea and fit calls return in place of the authoring call — net zero or one fewer).

## The defects, each a gate predicate or a renderer fix

1. **`multiattack2014` "or or"** — strip a leading "or " from each split name. Pin: the Pit Fiend's 2014 Multiattack. (step-6 code)
2. **2014 Spellcasting header ": -"** — the bullet strip also removes `:\s*-\s*$` on the header line. Pin: the Adult Green Dragon 2014.
3. **2014 conversion garbles on print sentences** — a `print` row's 2014 text is `to2014Grammar` of its 2024 text, never the model's; lint rule `conversion-dropped-half`: a 2024 row with "Half damage" whose 2014 row lacks "half as much damage" fails edition. Pin: Pit Fiend Bite, Noxious Miasma, Hellfire Spellcasting.
4. **The final price pass re-solves a print-named attack row** — a chassis row renamed to a print attack by the print-canon pass carries `print: true`. Pin: the Adult Green Dragon's Rend 15 (2d8 + 6); the Lich's Eldritch Burst 31 (4d8 + 13).
5. **`{dc}` without "DC"** — `renderForm` writes `DC {dc}` where the token is not already preceded by "DC"; an authored trigger whose subject is not the creature or "a creature" is rejected. Pin: the plague-seer's trait.
6. **A flavor sentence as a Traits row** — new adjudicability predicate `adjudicable-mechanic`: a Trait with no `GAME_TERM`, no number, no condition and no rule verb (can, can't, has, ignores, regains, moves) is flavor — fail; the parser routes it to `unplaced` → description. Pin: Bound Dead of the Kingdom.
7. **A non-striking noun as an attack row** — the attack form refuses stare, gaze, glare, look, eye, voice, aura, presence, scent, stench as names and re-files the form (a gaze is a save; a stench is a trait). Applies to the repair call; part rows come from the PART list and cannot produce it. Pin: the hermit's stare.
8. **Concept share on one-liners** — move 2 is the fix. Measure: the wyrm back to 7/9 or better, the herder to 4/5, the plague-seer's drain back.
9. **Route variance on "commands a stampede"** — leader one roll, charger the next. Named, not fixed here; if it recurs on the forty, `LINE_VERBS` for defender-leader gets "commands a".
10. **Self-referential cross-row reference** — the repair call's crossRef requires `payoffRow !== riderRow`. (The part-row pass writes no cross-row reference; the idea call may still exploit a part's rider, as the live Meshrin did.)

## Not in scope

The forms, the eight gates, the extraction and the reader's denominator, the header directives, the declared fields, the lacks removal and the route's negation rule stand. Print stand-down stays closed. No new calls. No change to the 2014 numbers, the bands or the print corpora.

## Pass conditions

- The twelve: print names zero unclassified against the release rolls, identity rows 6/6, template trace 6/6; one-liners zero unclassified and no verdict worse than the release — the row that decides each verdict named.
- Meshrin: Large; Speed 30; Greatsword, Tail Swipe (or the user's phrase), Razor Claws, Vicious Bite as attack rows; Multiattack naming all four; a row or rider for the fighting style; "Meshrin" throughout; all gates ok; coverage ≥ 7/8.
- The baseline set: 0 fails; coverage ≥ 85% on the reader's denominator; concept share ≥ 78%.
- The conversion set: the four 2014 defects absent; every other row identical to the twelve's run.
- The forty at close-out: 0 fails; coverage and concept share not below the release's (72% / 85%); no 2014 sheet with a doubled word, a stray dash or a dropped half clause; zero unclassified differences against the release rolls.
- Gate: `npx jest` and `npm run build` clean before any run; nothing lands while a run is in flight.
