# BLOCKS — the Kobold probe: six live rolls (2026-11-25)

`Kobold — Can turn invisible once per day`, CR 1/4, run live six times on the build at `79ad465d`,
before any code. The tapes are an experiment's and are kept outside the repo.

**The stop rule is clear: the route call declared `teleport` on 6 of 6.** R4 opens on the declared
ask, so widening it is worth doing.

**And the hole is not hit on any of the six.** `self-invisibility` led the offered menu every time —
it was never shelf-only — the fit call chose it every time, and every sheet carries a row that makes
the Kobold invisible. R4 never had to fire, and no seed was drawn. Fable's stand-in run showed the
hole by stamping the idea as an action AND having the fit decline; live, the fit did not decline.

One thing worth seeing: the fit call named its row six different ways — Vanishing Shimmer, Splinter
Vanish, Fading Scales, Vanish, Shed Scale Shimmer, Shimmer Scales — and all six shipped as
**Vanish (1/Day)**, the template's own name, under "one mechanic, one name".
