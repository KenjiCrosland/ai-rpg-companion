Traits

Legendary Resistance (3/Day). If the sunless king fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.

Spirit Jar. If destroyed, the sunless king reforms in 1d10 days if it has a spirit jar, reviving with all its Hit Points. The new body appears in an unoccupied space within the sunless king's lair.

Actions

Multiattack. The sunless king makes three attacks, using Hurled Scythe, Eldritch Burst, or Paralyzing Touch in any combination. It can replace one attack with a use of Binding Lash.

Hurled Scythe. Melee Attack Roll: +16, reach 5 ft. 45 (8d8 + 9) Necrotic damage.

Eldritch Burst. Melee Attack Roll: +16, reach 5 ft. 27 (4d8 + 9) Force damage.

Paralyzing Touch. Melee Attack Roll: +16, reach 5 ft. 27 (4d8 + 9) Cold damage, and the target has the Paralyzed condition until the start of the sunless king's next turn.

Spellcasting. The sunless king casts one of the following spells, using Intelligence as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 24):

- At Will: Detect Magic, Detect Thoughts, Dispel Magic, Fireball, Invisibility, Lightning Bolt, Mage Hand, Prestidigitation
- 2/Day Each: Animate Dead, Dimension Door, Plane Shift
- 1/Day Each: Chain Lightning, Finger of Death, Power Word Kill, Scrying

Binding Lash. Dexterity Saving Throw: DC 24, one creature within 60 feet. Failure: The target has the Restrained condition until the end of the sunless king's next turn.

Reactions

Protective Magic (3/Day). Trigger: The sunless king is hit by an attack roll. Response: The sunless king casts Shield in response, gaining a +5 bonus to AC until the start of its next turn, including against the triggering attack.

Legendary Actions

Legendary Action Uses: 3. Immediately after another creature's turn, the sunless king can expend a use to take one of the following actions. It regains all expended uses at the start of each of its turns.

Deathly Teleport. The sunless king teleports up to 60 feet to an unoccupied space it can see, and each creature within 10 feet of the space it left takes 11 (2d10) Necrotic damage.

Disrupt Life. Constitution Saving Throw: DC 24, each creature that isn't an Undead in a 20-foot Emanation originating from the sunless king. Failure: 31 (9d6) Necrotic damage. Success: Half damage. Failure or Success: the sunless king can't take this action again until the start of its next turn.

Frightening Gaze. The sunless king casts Fear, using the same spellcasting ability as Spellcasting. The sunless king can't take this action again until the start of its next turn.
