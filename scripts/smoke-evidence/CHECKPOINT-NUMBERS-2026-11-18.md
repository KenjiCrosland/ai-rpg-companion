- issue: The trail-erasing clause has no rules text defining what it does mechanically, so a GM cannot adjudicate what 'leaves no trail' means for tracking, scent, or pursuit. — Either cut the flavor clause or replace it with a defined benefit such as imposing Disadvantage on Wisdom (Survival) checks to track the affected creature.
