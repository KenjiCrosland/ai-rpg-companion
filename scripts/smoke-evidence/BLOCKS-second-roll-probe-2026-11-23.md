# BLOCKS — the second-roll probe (2026-11-23)

Twelve generations: the four sheets on the owner's Fix list, each rolled three times **live from the
idea call**, with every recorded answer before it served from
`baseline-2026-11-23-free-rows` — the forty as this round leaves it. The route, the chassis and the
name are the recorded ones; the ideas and everything after them are new.

**Each generation makes exactly one idea call**, so there is no question which one `--live-from idea`
took. (Checked in each run's own tape: 11 calls per generation, one of them the idea call.)

The recordings are an experiment's and stay out of the repo, under each run's gitignored `tape/`.

**Not graded.** Below each sheet is the recorded block for comparison, then the three rolls, then
only what the engine can say for itself: which seats landed a row, which were refused and asked
again, whether a stock seed was drawn, and the lint's findings.
