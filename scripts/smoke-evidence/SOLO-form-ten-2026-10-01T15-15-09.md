Chitin Plates. Melee Attack Roll: +8, reach 10 ft. 14 (2d8 + 5) Slashing damage plus 11 (2d10) Acid damage. If the target is a Large or smaller creature, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 16). While Grappled, the target has the Restrained condition.

Creeping Dread. Wisdom Saving Throw: DC 15, one creature within 60 feet. Failure: The target has the Frightened condition until the end of the glurr's next turn. If the target was already Frightened by this ability, it also takes 27 (6d8) Acid damage.

Bonus Actions

Engulf. The glurr engulfs one Medium or smaller creature it is grappling. While engulfed, the target has the Blinded and Restrained conditions, has Total Cover against attacks and other effects outside the glurr, and takes 10 (3d6) Acid damage at the start of each of the glurr's turns. The glurr can have only one creature engulfed at a time. If the glurr takes 25 damage or more on a single turn from the engulfed creature, the glurr must expel the target at the end of that turn, and the target exits Prone in an unoccupied space within 10 feet of the glurr. If the glurr dies, an engulfed creature pulls free of the mass Prone by using 10 feet of movement.
