## What landed

1. **The feature table** (`reskin/features.mjs`, ~15 entries, closed forms, no call). A named weapon earns one feature: a heavy weapon earns Great Weapon Master (+PB damage on a hit, as a trait), any other weapon its 2024 mastery property (Topple, Sap, Slow, Push, Vex as riders; Graze and Cleave as traits). A style word (fighting style, trained, disciplined, veteran, martial, technique) earns Trip Attack — a rider on the weapon row in print's own idiom ("the target is subjected to the following effect. *Strength Saving Throw*: DC n. *Failure:* The target has the Prone condition.") — and Riposte, a reaction. The six named styles and Sneak Attack have their own entries. The feature's real name is the row's name; the audit never renames a feature or a part. The condition-payoff template no longer keys on style words; on a weapon sheet the exploiter card is named Trip Attack and the riposte seed Riposte, and when the table already landed a Trip the template's copy leaves. Merciless Strike cannot ship under a style clause.
2. **Adamantine as a header idiom.** "Immune to anything but adamantine weapons" writes "Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing from nonmagical attacks that aren't adamantine" to Immunities (the 2014 card keeps "that aren't adamantine" through the normaliser). Never a trait, never the repair call. The user's own immunity line drives the DMG defensive read, so the bands gate reports `hot` as soft with "user-set" when it does.
3. **A swallow shape** (`shapes.mjs` → `swallow`, the Kraken's and Behir's grammar at the sheet's size and CR, six bands, for bodies with no engulf engine): "one Large or smaller creature within 10 feet that the creature hit with a melee attack this turn … swallowed … Restrained, Total Cover, Acid damage at the start of each turn … regurgitate on 30 damage in a turn". The 2024 Kraken's "must succeed on a DC n Constitution saving throw at the end of that turn" is print's idiom and the edition gate now knows it.
4. **The hag is a print borrow.** `hag`, `hags`, `crone` alias to the Night Hag; a Name prefix that is no print creature ("Grandmother Ash — An old hag …") no longer hides the head noun, while a print Name with an addition ("Goblin Boss — riding a worg") keeps the normal borrow read. On a borrow the user's parts, features and repair run (only an exact print name closes the row set); a part keeps its name through print's attack rename; the user's typed vulnerability removes the same type from a borrowed resistance; a print-named row keeps print's numbers only at print's CR and re-solves with the round above it.
5. **Coverage by provenance** (`checker.mjs`): a clause is covered when a row's trace binds to it — a part, feature or repaired row's own `clause`; a template row's `boundKeys` (the concept and idea keys its fit bound on, stamped at landing); an unplaced entry that carried it to the description; the chassis's own style word (brute-11-charger for "It charges"); a header line for a header clause; the head clause when any row exists. Never when a row's text happens to contain the clause's words. Pronouns and function words are not provenance (the hag's crows were counted covered through a shared "her").

Also landed off the runs: the Name field is the name when the model names none (Meshrin shipped once as "Meshrin Pit Champion"); a phobia trait shape ("afraid of", "flees from", with the fear as the slot) for Sir Reginald's chickens; "warn/alert" as a mechanical cue; "nails" as a part; the record's unplaced list is final after the repair, and a descriptive clause nothing claimed is filed to the description.
