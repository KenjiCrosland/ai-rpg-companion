Traits

Toll-Toothed Reckoning. The first time the tolltooth warden is Bloodied in a fight, it releases a burst. Constitution Saving Throw: DC 14, each creature in a 15-foot Emanation originating from the tolltooth warden. Failure: 24 (7d6) Bludgeoning damage, and the target has the Poisoned condition until the end of its next turn. Success: Half damage only.

Regeneration. The tolltooth warden regains 15 Hit Points at the start of each of its turns. If the tolltooth warden takes Acid or Fire damage, this trait doesn't function on the tolltooth warden's next turn. The tolltooth warden dies only if it starts its turn with 0 Hit Points and doesn't regenerate.

Actions

Multiattack. The tolltooth warden makes two Fists attacks.

Fists. Melee Attack Roll: +7, reach 5 ft. 26 (5d8 + 4) Bludgeoning damage. If the target is a Large or smaller creature, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 13). While Grappled, the target has the Restrained condition.

Toll-Payer's Mark (Recharge 5-6). Constitution Saving Throw: DC 14, each creature in a 20-foot Emanation originating from the tolltooth warden. Failure: 24 (7d6) Thunder damage, and the target has the Deafened condition until the end of its next turn. Success: Half damage only.

Bonus Actions

Swallow. The tolltooth warden swallows one Medium or smaller creature it is grappling. While swallowed, the target has the Blinded and Restrained conditions, has Total Cover against attacks and other effects outside the tolltooth warden, and takes 7 (2d6) Acid damage at the start of each of the tolltooth warden's turns. The tolltooth warden can have only one creature swallowed at a time. If the tolltooth warden takes 15 damage or more in a single turn from the swallowed creature, the tolltooth warden must regurgitate the target at the end of that turn, and the target exits Prone in an unoccupied space within 5 feet of the tolltooth warden. If the tolltooth warden dies, a swallowed creature exits the body Prone by using 5 feet of movement.
